# Cabbage and rice one-pot
## Ingredients
- cabbage
- rice
- carrot
- soy sauce
- oil
- water
## Steps
1. Wash and shred cabbage and carrot. Rinse uncooked rice.
2. Warm oil in a saucepan. Stir cabbage and carrot over medium heat for 4 minutes.
3. Add rice and the quantity of water specified on its package. Bring to a boil, cover, and cook according to the rice package.
4. Once rice is tender, stir in soy sauce and serve hot.
