# Chickpea, spinach and tomato pan
## Ingredients
- chickpeas
- spinach
- tomato
- garlic
- olive oil
- salt
## Steps
1. Drain cooked canned chickpeas, chop the tomato, and mince garlic.
2. Warm olive oil over medium heat. Stir the garlic for 30 seconds, then add tomato.
3. Cook for 5 minutes, then add chickpeas and a splash of water. Simmer until steaming hot.
4. Stir in washed spinach until wilted, season with salt, and serve.
