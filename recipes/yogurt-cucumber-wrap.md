# Cucumber yogurt chickpea wrap
## Ingredients
- chickpeas
- cucumber
- yogurt
- tortillas
- lemon
- salt
## Steps
1. Drain and rinse cooked canned chickpeas, then mash lightly with a fork.
2. Wash and dice cucumber. Stir it into the chickpeas with plain yogurt, lemon juice and salt.
3. Spoon the filling into fresh, non-moldy tortillas and fold into wraps.
4. Serve immediately; keep unused filling refrigerated.
