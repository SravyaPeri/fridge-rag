# Tomato cheese toast
## Ingredients
- bread
- tomato
- cheese
- black pepper
## Steps
1. Wash and slice the tomato. Toast slices of non-moldy bread.
2. Put the toast on a baking tray, add tomato slices, and top with cheese.
3. Broil while watching closely until the cheese melts and bubbles.
4. Finish with black pepper and let cool briefly before eating.
