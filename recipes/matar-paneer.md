# Matar paneer (paneer and green peas)
## Ingredients
- paneer
- green peas
- tomato
- onion
- garlic
- cumin
- oil
- water
- salt
## Steps
1. Dice 200 g of refrigerated paneer. Chop one onion and two tomatoes, and mince one garlic clove.
2. Warm a tablespoon of oil over medium heat. Add half a teaspoon of cumin, then the onion, and cook for 5 minutes until softened.
3. Stir in garlic for 30 seconds. Add the tomatoes and cook for 8 minutes, stirring, until softened into a thick sauce.
4. Add one cup of fresh or frozen green peas and half a cup of water. Simmer for 5-8 minutes until the peas are tender.
5. Add the paneer and simmer gently for 3-5 minutes until steaming hot throughout. Season with salt and serve. This recipe contains milk.
