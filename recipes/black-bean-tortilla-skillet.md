# Black bean tortilla skillet
## Ingredients
- black beans
- tortillas
- lime
- olive oil
- salt
## Steps
1. Drain and rinse cooked canned black beans. Cut safe, non-moldy tortillas into strips.
2. Warm a little olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Toast the strips until crisp, about 3 minutes.
3. Add the beans and a splash of water. Stir until the beans are steaming hot throughout, about 4 minutes.
4. Take off the heat, squeeze over lime, and season with salt. Serve immediately.
