# Banana oat pancakes
## Ingredients
- banana
- oats
- egg
- oil
## Steps
1. Mash a peeled ripe banana. Stir in one egg and enough oats to make a thick batter.
2. Let stand for 5 minutes so the oats soften.
3. Lightly oil a skillet over medium-low heat. Spoon small pancakes into the pan.
4. Cook for about 3 minutes per side until set throughout and the egg is fully cooked. Serve immediately.
