# Potato and egg breakfast hash
## Ingredients
- potato
- egg
- onion
- olive oil
- salt
## Steps
1. Wash and dice the potato into small cubes, and chop the onion.
2. Heat olive oil in a covered skillet over medium heat. Cook potato with a splash of water for 10 minutes.
3. Add onion and cook uncovered until the potato is tender and lightly golden.
4. Make a space in the pan and add the egg. Cover and cook until the white and yolk are firm. Season with salt.
