# Peanut carrot noodles
## Ingredients
- peanut butter
- noodles
- soy sauce
- carrot
- lime
- water
## Steps
1. Boil noodles according to their package directions and drain.
2. Whisk peanut butter with soy sauce, lime juice and a little warm water into a pourable sauce.
3. Wash and grate the carrot. Toss it with the hot noodles and sauce.
4. Serve warm. This recipe contains peanuts and may contain wheat and soy.
