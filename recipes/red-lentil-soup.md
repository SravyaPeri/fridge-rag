# Red lentil carrot soup
## Ingredients
- red lentils
- carrot
- onion
- cumin
- water
- olive oil
- salt
## Steps
1. Rinse red lentils. Wash and chop carrot and onion.
2. Soften onion in olive oil over medium heat for 5 minutes. Add carrot and a pinch of cumin.
3. Add lentils and enough water to cover generously. Bring to a boil, then simmer for 20 minutes until completely soft.
4. Mash lightly for a thicker soup and season with salt.
