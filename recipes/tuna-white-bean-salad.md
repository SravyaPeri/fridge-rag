# Tuna and white bean lemon salad
## Ingredients
- tuna
- white beans
- lemon
- cucumber
- olive oil
- black pepper
## Steps
1. Drain canned tuna and cooked canned white beans.
2. Wash and dice cucumber. Combine with the tuna and beans in a bowl.
3. Dress with olive oil, lemon juice and black pepper.
4. Serve immediately or refrigerate promptly. Use refrigerated leftovers within 3-4 days.
