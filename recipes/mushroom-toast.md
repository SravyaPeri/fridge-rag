# Garlic mushroom toast
## Ingredients
- mushrooms
- bread
- garlic
- butter
- salt
## Steps
1. Clean and slice mushrooms. Mince garlic.
2. Melt butter in a skillet over medium heat. Cook mushrooms for 7 minutes until softened and browned.
3. Add garlic and stir for 30 seconds. Season with salt.
4. Toast bread and spoon the hot mushrooms over it. Use only bread with no mold.
