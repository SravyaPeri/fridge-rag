# Lemon broccoli pasta
## Ingredients
- pasta
- broccoli
- lemon
- garlic
- olive oil
- salt
## Steps
1. Boil pasta following its package directions. Add washed broccoli florets for the final 4 minutes.
2. Reserve a small mug of cooking water, then drain the pasta and broccoli.
3. In the empty pot, warm olive oil with minced garlic for 30 seconds.
4. Toss in the pasta, broccoli and lemon juice. Add a little reserved water to loosen and season.
